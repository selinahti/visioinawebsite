*Last updated: 30 July 2026*

This notice is about the **website** at visioina.com. Our apps are separate and
have their own policies — see the [OTUS Privacy Policy]({{ '/apps/otus/privacy/' | relative_url }}).

## The short version

This site is a set of plain files. It sets no cookies, stores nothing on your
device, runs no analytics, loads no fonts or scripts from other companies, and
embeds nothing from anywhere else. There is no consent banner because there is
nothing to consent to.

## Cookies

None. The site sets no cookies of any kind — not for analytics, not for
advertising, not for preferences. Nothing is written to your browser's storage
either.

## Server logs

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. Like every web
server, GitHub's servers record requests as they are served: your IP address,
the page requested, the time, and your browser's user-agent string. This happens
at the hosting level, is used to deliver the site and to protect it from abuse,
and is kept for a short period by GitHub. We do not receive these logs, do not
analyse them, and cannot identify individual visitors from them. The legal basis
is our legitimate interest in running a secure, working website.

GitHub's own privacy practices are described in the
[GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement).

## If you email us

If you write to us, we receive your email address and whatever you put in your
message. We use it only to reply, and keep it only as long as the conversation
is useful — support threads are deleted once they are clearly finished. We do
not add anyone to a mailing list and do not pass messages to anyone else.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you may ask us what personal data we hold about you, have it
corrected or deleted, object to our processing it, or ask for a copy. In
practice we hold nothing about website visitors beyond any email you have sent
us. Write to [{{ site.company.email }}](mailto:{{ site.company.email }}) and we
will answer.

If you think we have handled your data incorrectly, you may complain to the
Finnish Data Protection Ombudsman
([tietosuoja.fi](https://tietosuoja.fi/en/home)).

## Changes
