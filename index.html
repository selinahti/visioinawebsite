Apps that make life better.

Visioina is a Finnish app studio. We make apps and games that are genuinely useful, delightful, or — on a good day — both: things that help you focus, get more done, and have more fun doing it. Our first app, OTUS, is coming soon.

Who we are

A small studio in Finland

Visioina is an independent studio based in Espoo. We build software the way we would want it built for us and care about the details nobody asked us to care about.

🎯

Apps on point

An app should be explainable in a sentence. So no nonsense, just apps that works how we would personally want to use them.

🎮

Useful or fun (both!)

Productivity tools do not have to feel like homework, and games can do more than pass the time. We build in the space where the two meet —apps with a sense of humour, games with a point.

🔐

Privacy taken seriously

We ask for as little as an app needs to work, and every app says plainly what it stores and why. OTUS is built privacy-first: yourgalaxy lives on your iPhone and in your own iCloud.
