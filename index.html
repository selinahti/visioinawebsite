Who we are

Deliberately small

Being small is the point. It means we can build software the way we would want it built for us, take the slow option when the slow option is better, and answer our own email.

🎯

One idea per app

An app should be explainable in a sentence. We would rather ship something narrow that works beautifully than something broad that works adequately, and we leave features out on purpose.

🔐

Nothing collected

No accounts, no analytics SDKs, no advertising, no tracking. Your data lives on your iPhone and, if you want it to, in your own private iCloud. We run no servers, so we could not read it even if we wanted to.

✉️

A person answers

Support is an email address, not a ticket queue or a chatbot. If you write to us about a bug, the reply comes from someone who can go and fix it.
